# Daylight environment

Reference generated first with the built-in imagegen tool, saved as `daylight-reference.png`.

Prompt: Use case: stylized-concept. Art direction reference for an existing colorful 3D medieval island town game inspired by Clash of Clans. Wide landscape three-quarter aerial view, 19 well-spaced timber cottages with orange roofs, blue-roofed town hall, curved paths, lush green island and small harbor. Primary focus is a joyful cohesive environment: clear pastel azure sky, soft sculpted white cumulus clouds overhead near the horizon, one warm sun upper left casting consistent soft directional shadows, luminous turquoise and aqua ocean, pale mint shallows blending into sandy beaches, no dark navy water. Soft ambient sky bounce, warm stone and timber, bright clean mobile strategy game aesthetic, harmonious moderate saturation. Entire world feels part of one sunlit environment, water softly stylized with gentle highlights rather than harsh white glitter. Show sky above a low distant horizon and island filling lower two thirds. No text or UI. Feasible in procedural Three.js.

Implementation: one warm directional sun supplies all directional light and shadows. Hemisphere illumination represents diffuse sky and ground bounce; the extra ambient lamp has been removed. The sky's sun and water highlights share the exact same sun direction. Puffy procedural clouds drift slowly outside the village and respect reduced motion. Azure sky, pale atmospheric horizon, turquoise water, mint shallows and sandy shoreline use a shared tone-mapping and sRGB output pipeline. Reduced ocean normal strength, wave amplitude, noise contrast and specular strength remove the old harsh glitter. A slightly lower opening camera angle shows the clouds and distance while keeping the town readable. The simulation's dusk effect remains a subtle warm daylight change.
